Privacy policy · Am I on Camera?

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short

We don't ask you to sign up, we don't run our own servers and we don't sell your data. What the app uses stays on your phone, except what the Google services listed below process.

Who publishes it

Am I on Camera? is an app by Ganddda Studio, an independent developer based in Mexico. If you have questions about this policy, write to gandddastudio@gmail.com.

What the app uses and why

  • Camera and flashlight: to detect lens reflections. The image is analyzed on your phone and is not recorded or sent.
  • Local Wi-Fi network: the scan looks for devices on your same network (for example, cameras). Results stay on your phone and are never sent to a server.
  • Sensors: the magnetometer helps the search.
  • Room history: stored only on your phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, phone model and ad interaction data, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Where the law requires it (for example, in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Ganddda Studio never receives your name or card details; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase or subscription is active. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in Google Play.

Sharing

Images or cards are only shared when you tap “Share”, with the app you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

How to delete your data

Because your data lives on your phone, it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. For ad data, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.
