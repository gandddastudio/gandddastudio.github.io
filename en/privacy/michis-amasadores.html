Privacy policy · Kneady Cats!

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short

We don't ask you to sign up, we don't run our own servers and we don't sell your data. What the app uses stays on your phone, except what the Google services listed below process.

Who publishes it

Kneady Cats! is an app by Ganddda Studio, an independent developer based in Mexico. If you have questions about this policy, write to gandddastudio@gmail.com.

What the app uses and why

  • Your cat's photo: if you add your cat, you pick the photo from your gallery. It is stored only on your phone.
  • Notifications: to let you know about lives or challenges, if you turn them on.
  • Progress and collection: stored only on your phone.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Ganddda Studio never receives your name or card details; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase or subscription is active. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in Google Play.

Sharing

Images or cards are only shared when you tap “Share”, with the app you choose.

Children

Kneady Cats! is suitable for children and follows the Google Play Families policy. All ads are requested as child-directed: they are not personalized, are rated for all audiences and do not use the advertising ID to build profiles. We don't ask for a name, email or location. Your cat's photo stays on the phone. Purchases require authorization from the device's Google Play account; we recommend turning on purchase approval in Google Family Link. Parents or guardians can write to us at any time.

How to delete your data

Because your data lives on your phone, it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. For ad data, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.
